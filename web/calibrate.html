1 · Head

Put the headset on the way you'll play, press Calibrate head and follow the big prompts (~30 s). It learns how your nod and head-turn look to the sensor and how much it drifts. Redo it whenever the headset shifts on your head.

Point mode needs step 1b; until then the cursor works like a joystick. Smoothing: lower = steadier when still. Speed boost: higher = less lag on quick moves.

1b · Comfortable range

      
After Calibrate head (or Re-center): tilt as far as is comfortable, left, right, down, then up a little, and hold each for 2 s. The cursor then reaches the screen edge at that tilt, so small neck movements are enough.
