1 · Head

Sit still looking at the screen and press Zero. Then tilt your head right and down: the dot should go right and down. If it doesn't, change the axes.

Point mode needs step 1b; until then the cursor works like a joystick. Smoothing: lower = steadier when still. Speed boost: higher = less lag on quick moves.


    


    

      
1b · Comfortable range

      
After Zero: tilt as far as is comfortable, left, right, down, then up a little, and hold each for 2 s. The cursor then reaches the screen edge at that tilt, so small neck movements are enough.
